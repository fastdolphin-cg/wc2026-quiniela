📖 Fast Dolphin World Cup 2026 Tournament Rules

Everything you need to know about how to play and how points are calculated

⚽ How It Works
01
Predict Every Match
You predict the final score of all 104 World Cup matches — from the Group Stage all the way to the Final. Each match must be predicted individually before it kicks off.
02
Picks Lock at Kickoff
Once a match begins, your prediction is locked and cannot be changed. Make sure to submit your pick before the referee blows the whistle!
03
Knockout Rounds
For knockout matches, you predict the final result including extra time and penalties. A match that ends 1–1 after 90 minutes but goes to penalties counts as the penalty winner's result.
04
Automatic Scoring
Points are calculated automatically as results come in. The leaderboard updates in real time so you always know where you stand in the competition.
05
Privacy
Your picks are private and invisible to other participants until a match kicks off. Once the game starts, everyone can see what you predicted.
06
Prizes 🎁
Cash prizes will be awarded to the top 3 finishers at the end of the tournament. Details to be announced. Play well and good luck!

📅 Tournament Stages
📋
Group Stage
72 matches across 12 groups (June 11–27). Each team plays 3 matches. Top 2 from each group advance, plus the 8 best 3rd-place teams = 32 teams advance.
⚔️
Round of 32
June 28 – July 3. The 32 qualified teams face off. Picks open automatically once both teams are confirmed for each match.

Round of 16 → Final
